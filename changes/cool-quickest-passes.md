### Added

- Corners tab: a "Quickest passes" view beside "Lap N" — per corner your quickest time, its lap
  and how many laps are level with it (the demo's C5: 3.52 ★ on lap 10, the next +0.071 s).
- Click a row (or ▶, or Return) to play that pass: 2 s before the corner to 1 s after it, then it
  pauses. Click the same row again for the next level lap; ⌘K lists "Quickest C3 — lap 13".
