### Changed

- Stats ▸ CORNERS marks the corners where your passes vary most and cost most.
- It ranks them by middle half × median loss, not σ × loss: one unusual pass no longer earns it.
- The σ hover names the one pass when a single pass carries most of a corner's σ.
- The mark is now ◆ instead of ▲, because ▲ means "ahead" in every Δ readout.
- No mark under 5 counted passes, or on a middle half under 0.07 s, the level step.
