### Changed

- Stats ▸ CORNERS marks its erratic-and-slow corners by middle half × median loss, not σ × loss.
- One unusual pass no longer earns a corner the mark; the σ hover names that pass instead.
- The mark is now ◆ instead of ▲, because ▲ means "ahead" in every Δ readout.
- A corner with fewer than 5 counted passes is never marked.
